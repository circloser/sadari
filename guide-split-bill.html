How to Split a Bill Fairly (Dutch Pay)

A free online bill splitter — no sign-up, works in any browser.

Splitting the check after a group dinner shouldn't be stressful. Whether you want a perfectly even split or a fun random "who pays" game, SADARI divides a shared bill among any number of people in seconds and lets everyone see the result instantly.

Two ways people split a bill

  • Even (Dutch pay): everyone pays the same share. Simple and fair for most outings.
  • Random: a ladder game decides who pays what — a popular way to add suspense, where some "win" (pay nothing) and others share the total.

Split a bill in 4 steps

  1. Open the ladder game and select the Money split mode.
  2. Enter the number of people and the total amount (8 currencies supported: USD, EUR, JPY, KRW, and more).
  3. Press run — the total is distributed automatically in clean currency units, with low variance so no one pays far more than others.
  4. Share the result with a link, KakaoTalk, WhatsApp/Telegram, or by copying the text or image.

Tips for a smooth split

  • Agree on tax and tip before drawing, then enter the grand total.
  • For uneven cases (e.g., someone didn't drink), set fewer "winners" so they pay less.
  • Send the result link so everyone can verify the outcome themselves.
