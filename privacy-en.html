Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 28, 2026

SADARI ("the Site") respects your privacy. The Site has no sign-up and does not collect or store personal information such as your name or contact details on any server.

1. Information we handle

  • Data you enter: participant names entered in the ladder/seating tools are stored only in your browser (LocalStorage) and are never sent to a server.
  • Preferences: language, currency, dark/light mode, and sound settings are stored only in your browser.
  • Automatically collected: for advertising and traffic analytics, third-party services may collect cookies, IP address, browser type, and visit history (see sections 3–4).

2. Cookies

The Site may use cookies and similar technologies (such as LocalStorage) for convenience features and ad delivery. You can refuse or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some features may then be limited.

3. Advertising & Google AdSense

The Site uses (or plans to use) Google AdSense to display ads.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google uses advertising cookies (e.g., the DoubleClick DART cookie).
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising via Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of third-party vendor cookies at www.aboutads.info.
  • For details, see Google's advertising policies.

4. Third-party services

  • Google AdSense — advertising (uses cookies)
  • Kakao JavaScript SDK — KakaoTalk sharing; runs only when you click the share button
  • Cloudflare — hosting and CDN; access logs may be processed for security/traffic purposes

5. Children

The Site is not directed to children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

6. Your choices

Because the Site stores no personal data on a server, any data kept in your browser can be cleared at any time via your browser settings (clear cookies / site data).

7. Changes

This policy may be updated to reflect legal or service changes; updates will be posted on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, email singlena@gmail.com or use the Contact page.
