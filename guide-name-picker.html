Running a giveaway, choosing who goes first, or picking a lucky winner? A random name picker makes the choice fair and fun. SADARI offers three ways to draw — a spinning roulette wheel, a card draw, and the classic ladder game — all free and instant.

Pick a winner in 3 steps

  1. Enter or paste the names (one per line or comma-separated).
