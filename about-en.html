About

SADARI is a free online ladder game (Amidakuji / Ghost Leg) and all-in-one random picker that anyone can use. No install, no sign-up — it runs right in your web browser.

Our purpose

We make group decisions fair and fun: things that classic ladder games handled poorly, like forming teams for large groups and automatically splitting a shared bill (Dutch pay), plus everyday random choices.

Features

  • 🪜 Ladder game — up to 50 players, adjustable complexity, diagonal rungs, animation, Win/Lose · team formation · money distribution
  • 🎡 Roulette · 🎫 Draw lots · 🪙 Coin flip · 🎲 Dice · 💺 Seating · ⏱️ Timer
  • Share results via link, KakaoTalk, text, or image
  • 9 languages: Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese, Spanish, German, French, Russian, Portuguese
  • Dark/Light mode, sound effects, background music, bulk-paste names, saved rosters

Original content

Beyond the tool, SADARI writes and publishes original editorial articles about the ladder game itself: its 700-year history (Muromachi-era Amidakuji), the math that makes it always fair (permutations and one-to-one correspondence), a comparison of fair drawing methods, and practical uses. Read them in our articles.

Who runs it & our principles

SADARI is an independent web service, designed, built and operated by a solo developer, launched in 2026. We follow these principles:

  • Free, no install, no sign-up — every feature is available without an account.
  • Minimal data collection — the names you enter stay on your device (browser) and are never sent to our server unless you explicitly create a share link.
  • Clear separation of content and ads — ads may be shown to cover running costs, but they are kept clearly distinct from the content.
