Random Team Generator

Split any group into fair, random teams — free, up to 30 people.

Need to divide a class, a sports group, or a workshop into teams without arguments? A random team generator assigns everyone fairly in one tap. SADARI splits your group into the number of teams you choose, evenly and at random, and lets you share the result instantly.

Generate teams in 4 steps

  1. Enter the participants. You can bulk-paste a list (commas or new lines) instead of typing one by one.
  2. Choose the Teams result mode and set the number of teams.
  3. Run the ladder — players are distributed evenly across teams at random.
  4. Share the teams via link, KakaoTalk, WhatsApp, Telegram, or X.

Why use a ladder for teams?

Unlike a plain shuffle, the animated ladder makes the draw feel fair and transparent — everyone watches the outcome unfold, which reduces "that's rigged" complaints. You can also re-shuffle instantly for a fresh draw.

Other ways to group people

  • Seating tool — assign names to a seating grid at random.
  • Roulette / draw lots — pick captains first, then build teams around them.
