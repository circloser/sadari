Wheel of Names

Enter names, spin the wheel, pick a random winner — free, no sign-up.

A wheel of names (also called a spinner wheel or picker wheel) is the fun, fair way to choose a random winner. Type in the names, give it a spin, and watch it slow to a stop on one lucky pick. SADARI's wheel is free, instant, and works right in your browser — great for giveaways, classrooms, raffles, and deciding who goes first.

How to spin in 3 steps

  1. Enter or paste the names onto the wheel (one per line or comma-separated).
  2. Tap Spin and let the wheel slow to a random winner.
  3. Reveal the winner — remove them and spin again for the next, or share by link or image.
