Ghost Leg / Amidakuji Online

Enter names, hit start — the ladder draws itself. Up to 50 players, no sign-up.

Ghost Leg — called Amidakuji (あみだくじ) in Japan and Sadari-taki (사다리타기) in Korea — is a classic lottery game: vertical lines linked by random horizontal rungs, where following each line from top to bottom leads to a random outcome. SADARI lets you play it online in seconds: no paper, no pen, no counting mistakes.

Play in 3 steps

  1. Enter names — type them in or paste a list (comma or line separated).
  2. Set the outcomes — Win/Lose, running order, teams, or a total amount to split.
  3. Hit start — the ladder is generated randomly and each path is traced with animation. Don't like it? Shuffle and redraw.

What people use it for

  • Deciding order — presentation, karaoke, or turn order, fairly.
  • Who pays / penalties — pick who buys the round or does the dare.
  • Teams & seating — assign people to teams or a seating grid at random.
  • Splitting a bill — enter a total and it's distributed automatically (22 currencies).

Why an online ladder beats paper

A hand-drawn ladder is easy to rig and tedious to trace. SADARI draws the rungs randomly and animates every path, so it's fair and transparent. Adjust the number of players and line complexity freely, then share the result as a link or on social media.
